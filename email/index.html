Add it to Outlook
  1. Fill in your details on the left and check the preview above.
  2. Click Copy signature.
  3. Open Outlook. In the menu bar at the top of the screen, click Outlook, then Settings.
  4. Click Signatures.
  5. If you have an old signature, click it, then click the minus (–) button to delete it.
  6. Click the plus (+) button to add a new signature and name it ARE.
  7. Click into the large box and press Cmd+V to paste.
  8. Below the box, set New messages to ARE and Replies/forwards to ARE.
  9. Close the Signatures window. It saves automatically.

  1. Fill in your details on the left and check the preview above.
  2. Click Copy signature.
  3. In New Outlook or Outlook on the web, click the gear icon (Settings) in the top right.
  4. Click Accounts, then Signatures.
  5. If you have an old signature, select it and delete it.
  6. Click + New signature and name it ARE.
  7. Click into the large box and paste (Ctrl+V on Windows, Cmd+V on Mac).
  8. Under Default signatures, set both For new messages and For replies/forwards to ARE.
  9. Click Save.

Test it: send an email to yourself or a coworker to check that it looks right.

Logo shows as a gray box? That's your own Outlook blocking pictures. Click Download pictures at the top of the email. The people you email will see the logo normally.

Phone: the Outlook app on your phone has its own signature setting and doesn't use this one. Plain text there is fine.

Layout came out scrambled after pasting? Click Copy HTML code instead and send it to Jeff Needham for help.
